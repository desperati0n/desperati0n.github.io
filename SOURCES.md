- `21st/7611-Hero` — animated dark hero and marquee architecture
- `21st/8324-Bento-Monochrome` — monochrome dense bento grid
- `21st/13553-One-Accordion` — expanding accordion interaction
- `21st/1855-Commits-Grid` — GitHub-style contribution grid text effect, MIT
- `21st/9481-Framer-Carousel` — animated carousel transitions
- `21st/952-Sticky-Scroll-Reveal` — sticky scroll and reveal composition
- `21st/11714-Motion-Footer` — cinematic motion footer
- `21st/17789-Liquid-Metal` — animated WebGL hero background, Apache-2.0
- `21st/5639-Particles-BG` — interactive mouse-connected particle background, MIT

The content, palette, accessibility labels, and responsive behavior were adapted for a computer science student portfolio.
